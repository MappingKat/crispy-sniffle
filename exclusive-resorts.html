01 How I'd unify the Collective

The integration play, in four moves.

"Move fluidly across brands" is the strategic promise. It's also, structurally, a cross-brand identity + lifecycle problem. Here's how I'd build it.

Move 01 · Map the three stacks

Audit ER, Inspirato, onefinestay end-to-end

CRM, marketing automation, CMS, attribution, service-side ambassador tooling. Find the overlap (a member's email lives in three databases), find the seams (no cross-brand event tracking), name what stays vs. what gets consolidated.

Move 02 · Cross-brand identity resolution

Recognize members across the Collective

A onefinestay renter as an Inspirato candidate. An Inspirato subscriber whose six-month pattern looks like a future ER member. I did exactly this at Lytics for Whirlpool — translated relational customer tables into a graph identity model, with tiered-confidence scoring on every link.

Move 03 · Lifecycle, right-time + right-brand

Orchestrate the journey across brands

With the identity layer in place, the lifecycle becomes orchestrate-able. Trigger an Inspirato upgrade nudge when onefinestay behavior matches the profile. Trigger an ER referral conversation when an Inspirato trend matches the member profile. The system knows which brand to talk from, at what moment.

Move 04 · AI member intelligence

Ambassador-side LLM briefing layer

Each ER member has a dedicated ambassador. Their context lives in scattered service notes. A Claude-powered briefing turns that into a structured pre-trip brief — history, preferences, travel patterns, who they're traveling with. Scales "anticipate before they ask" without adding headcount.

The deliverable is a Collective that actually works like one — and a marketing org that ships once across three brands instead of three times across one.
