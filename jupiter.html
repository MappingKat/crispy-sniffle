I'm Katrina Engelsted, and I'm excited to apply for the Principal, Customer Success role. My background sits at the intersection of geospatial technology, data, and customer-facing work. At Zayo Group, I also managed a Salesforce marketing stack, including Einstein and Marketing Cloud.

My previous titles haven't always said "customer success," but the work has consistently involved the same core responsibilities: building trust, understanding what a customer is trying to accomplish, working through technical questions, and making sure the product is useful in practice. That's the kind of work I enjoy, and climate-risk analytics is a product with a clear, meaningful purpose behind it.
