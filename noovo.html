01 Receipt #1 · The wellness van business

I co-founded Elevated Van Journeys.

For my Quantic MBA capstone, my co-founders and I built a complete business plan for Elevated Van Journeys — a wellness tourism company offering turnkey electric-van excursions through national parks, with guided audio experiences and evening community gatherings along the route.

Three co-founders, three skill sets: a licensed marriage and family therapist for the wellness programming, a mechanical engineer who'd already converted two vans, and me on product, tech, and operations.

  • The experience: electric vans + curated routes + nightly hubs at majestic locations like our national parks
  • The wellness layer: guided audio podcasts on mindfulness, relationships in nature, and self-reflection — playable from the van's sound system
  • The community layer: support staff and intentional evening gatherings at each hub stop
  • The thesis: wellness tourists spend 178% more than typical domestic travelers — we'd give them turnkey access to a market that didn't exist yet
  • The model: Blue Ocean strategy combining wellness retreats, RV rentals, camping, and psychotherapy under one brand
